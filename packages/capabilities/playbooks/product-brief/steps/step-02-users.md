# Step 2: The users

Ask, one at a time:
1. Who is the main user? Describe one real person or a typical day.
2. Who else is affected (buyers, approvers, support, people who see the output)?
3. What does the main user care about most: speed, price, trust, control?
4. What would make them stop using it?

Write the Users section with one primary and up to two secondary groups.
