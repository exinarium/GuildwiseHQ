# Step 3: The solution

Ask, one at a time:
1. In one sentence, what is the thing and what does it let the user do?
2. Walk me through the first use, from start to the moment they get value.
3. What makes this better than what they do now?
4. What is the single most important thing it must get right?

Write the Solution section. If the owner has several directions, record them as options and ask which to carry forward.
