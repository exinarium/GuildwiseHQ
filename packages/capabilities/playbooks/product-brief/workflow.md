# Product brief

Goal: a short brief the owner can hand to others, built from the owner's own answers.

Rules for the facilitator:
- Ask one question at a time with quick-pick options and free text. Allow skip and park.
- Never invent facts. Mark anything unconfirmed as an assumption.
- Update the brief after each step and show the section you just changed.
- Read each step with `aom skill show playbook:product-brief --file steps/<name>`.

Steps:
1. `steps/step-01-problem.md`
2. `steps/step-02-users.md`
3. `steps/step-03-solution.md`
4. `steps/step-04-scope.md`
5. `steps/step-05-measures-risks.md`
6. `steps/step-06-review.md`

The brief has these headings: Summary, Problem, Users, Solution, Scope (in and out), Success measures, Risks and open
questions, Next steps.
