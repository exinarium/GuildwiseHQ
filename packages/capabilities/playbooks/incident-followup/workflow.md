# Incident follow-up

Goal: the team learns from the incident and leaves with a few actions that have an owner and a date.

Rules for the facilitator:
- Ask one question at a time. Allow skip and park.
- Blameless: describe what the system and the process allowed, never who was careless. Name roles, not blame.
- Separate what is known from what is guessed; mark guesses as hypotheses.
- Limit actions to what the team will really do; a long list is a list nobody does.
- Read each step with `aom skill show playbook:incident-followup --file steps/<name>`.

Steps:
1. `steps/step-01-timeline.md`
2. `steps/step-02-causes.md`
3. `steps/step-03-helped.md`
4. `steps/step-04-actions.md`

The document has these headings: Summary, Impact, Timeline, Causes, What helped, What we will change (actions with owner
and date), Open questions.
