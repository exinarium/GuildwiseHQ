# Step 4: Actions

Propose at most five actions, each tied to a cause, and for each ask who owns it and by when. Prefer actions that remove the
cause over actions that ask people to be more careful.

Write What we will change as a list of action, owner and date. List what is still unknown under Open questions. When the owner
approves, save the document and offer a three-line message for the people who were affected.
