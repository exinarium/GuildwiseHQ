# Brainstorm

Goal: leave the session with a ranked shortlist of ideas the owner has chosen, not a pile of notes.

Rules for the facilitator:
- Run the steps in order. Read the step file with `aom skill show playbook:brainstorm --file steps/<name>`.
- The technique catalogue is `assets/techniques.csv` (`category,technique_name,description`). Pick two or three that fit
  the topic and the owner's energy. Do not run more than three.
- One question or prompt at a time. Quantity first, judgement later.
- Never score or reject an idea in the diverge step.
- Record every idea, even rough ones, in the session's idea list.

Steps:
1. `steps/step-01-setup.md`
2. `steps/step-02-diverge.md`
3. `steps/step-03-converge.md`
4. `steps/step-04-capture.md`
