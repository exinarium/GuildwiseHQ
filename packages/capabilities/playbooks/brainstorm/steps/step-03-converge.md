# Step 3: Converge

1. Group the ideas by theme. Propose the groups and let the owner move ideas between them.
2. Merge duplicates, keeping both wordings in the note.
3. Ask the owner to pick their top three groups, then their top three ideas inside each. Use quick-pick options.
4. For each chosen idea ask the same three questions: what does it solve, what would it take, what could go wrong.
5. Rank the shortlist with the owner. Put anything that did not make the cut in the parked list rather than deleting it.
