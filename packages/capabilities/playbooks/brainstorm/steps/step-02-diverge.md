# Step 2: Diverge

For each chosen technique:
1. Explain it in two sentences and give one example for this topic.
2. Prompt the owner for ideas, one prompt at a time. Offer your own ideas only after they have given theirs, or when asked.
3. Record every idea in the idea list, using the owner's words.
4. After about five minutes or ten ideas, ask "What else?" once, then move on.

Stay generative. If the owner judges an idea, record the concern as a note on the idea and keep going.
At the end of the step, read the full list back and ask if anything is missing.
