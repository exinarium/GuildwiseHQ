# Step 4: Capture

1. Write the shortlist as a ranked list: idea, why it matters, first small step, main risk.
2. List the open questions and the assumptions that the shortlist depends on.
3. Ask which item the owner wants to take forward and in what form (a brief, a PRD, a boardroom debate, or nothing yet).
4. Save the result to the session document as a suggestion for the owner to apply.
