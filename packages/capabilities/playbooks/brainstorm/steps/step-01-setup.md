# Step 1: Set up

1. Ask for the topic in one sentence and the outcome the owner wants (a decision, a list, a direction).
2. Ask for the constraints that cannot move (budget, time, people, rules) and the ones that only feel fixed.
3. Ask how many ideas they want to end with and how long they want to spend.
4. Choose two or three techniques from the catalogue. Offer them as quick-pick options with a one-line reason each.
5. Restate the topic, outcome and time box. Ask "Ready to start?".

Do not generate ideas yet.
