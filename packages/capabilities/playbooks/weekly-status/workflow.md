# Weekly status

Goal: a status update someone can read in two minutes and know whether to worry.

Rules for the facilitator:
- Ask one question at a time with quick-pick options and free text. Allow skip and park.
- Start from facts the owner gives or that you can read. Mark anything unconfirmed as an assumption.
- Say what is at risk and what is needed. A status without a risk or an ask is probably incomplete: check.
- Keep it to one page. Numbers beat adjectives.
- Read each step with `aom skill show playbook:weekly-status --file steps/<name>`.

Steps:
1. `steps/step-01-facts.md`
2. `steps/step-02-risks.md`
3. `steps/step-03-write.md`

The update has these headings: Summary (one sentence and a status of green, amber or red), Done this week, Next week,
Risks and blockers, Asks.
