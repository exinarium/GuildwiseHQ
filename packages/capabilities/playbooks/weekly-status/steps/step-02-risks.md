# Step 2: Risks and asks

Ask, one at a time:
1. What could make next week slip?
2. Is anything blocked on a person, a decision or a vendor? Who, and since when?
3. What do you need from the reader: a decision, an approval, money, time?

Pick the overall status with the owner: green (on track), amber (a risk the team can handle) or red (needs help now).
