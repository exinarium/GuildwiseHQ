# Step 3: Propose changes

1. Turn each finding that deserves action into a concrete edit to the draft: the text to change and the new text.
2. Present the edits as a numbered list, each with its reason.
3. Ask the owner to choose: Apply (all or a numbered subset), Reject, or Redirect (say what to do instead).
4. Apply only what was approved, and record rejected edits as decisions with a reason.
5. Offer another method or finish.
