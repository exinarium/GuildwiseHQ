# Step 1: Collect what shipped

Ask, one at a time:
1. Which release is this, and which date or version range does it cover?
2. Where is the list of changes: pasted text, a list of cards, or merged pull requests the owner can name?
3. Who is the reader: customers, the team, or both?

List every change in one line each, as the owner gave it. Do not rewrite yet. Ask the owner to add anything missing.
