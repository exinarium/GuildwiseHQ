Platform > Blog has a new Videos tab. Articles, releases and tutorials become narrated videos (and Shorts) that wait for your review; you watch the rendered video with its captions, edit the script and details, and approve it. Approved videos upload to your YouTube channel at 10:00 Johannesburg time, one a day. Connect the channel under Social sharing and switch Videos on; nothing happens before that. You can also import the existing help videos as tutorials and keep a list of ideas for new showcase videos.
