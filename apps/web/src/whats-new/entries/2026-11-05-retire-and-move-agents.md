title: Retire a workstation and move its agents
date: 2026-11-05
audience: admin
help: /help/workstations-and-installer#retiring
release: 1.5.0
---
**Retire** on the Workstations page moves a workstation's agents to another one, or marks them as needing a new home. **Move all agents** does the same for an offline or removed workstation, and any agent can be moved from its card on the Agents page or the Org chart.
