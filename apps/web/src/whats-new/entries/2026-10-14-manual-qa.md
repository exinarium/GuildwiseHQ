---
title: Test a card before it is done (manual QA)
date: 2026-10-14
audience: all
help: /help/testing-and-finalization#manual-qa
---
A finalized card now goes to **QA** before it is done: the person who started it gets a desk item and an agent writes test steps (happy path, edge cases, errors, regressions) on the card. They can **pass** it, **skip** the test, **send it back** with a comment, or give it to someone else. A failed test sends the card back to rework and through finalize and QA again, so you no longer reopen closed cards. The update on the Jira or GitHub ticket is held until the card passes or the test is skipped. It is on for every project; turn it off in the project's settings. Cards already in progress follow the setting when they are finalized.
