A Short can now be posted to the networks you pick when you schedule it, at the same slot as on YouTube: Facebook and Instagram Reels, X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and TikTok. Connect Instagram (the account id and the Page token) and TikTok (a Connect button) under Social sharing, and give each Short a caption per network. If a network fails, the Queue shows a retry button for that network only. TikTok posts privately until its audit passes; tick that in the TikTok row once it has.
