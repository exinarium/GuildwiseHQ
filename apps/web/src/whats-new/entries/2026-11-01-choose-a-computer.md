title: Choose a computer when one was removed
date: 2026-11-01
audience: admin
help: /help/phases
release: 1.4.0
---
When you remove a computer, its test and live environments no longer break. Before you confirm, Guildwise lists the environments that will need another computer, and afterwards each one says its computer was removed and offers **Choose a computer** to carry on from one of yours.
