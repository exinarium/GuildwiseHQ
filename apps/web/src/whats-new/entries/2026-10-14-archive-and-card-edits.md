date: 2026-10-14
audience: all

---
Done cards leave the board a day after they are finished and move to the project's **Archive** (link on the board): search it, filter it, open a card or reopen it. You can now change a card's **priority** and **completion action** at any time, even while an agent works on it. The board shows the real **spend this month** also when the project has no budget. The BA no longer reads code or sets how a card lands: it hands code questions to the Architect, and only you change the completion action.
