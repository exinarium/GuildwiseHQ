---
title: Agents read the knowledge vault in chats
date: 2026-10-13
audience: all
help: /help/ledger-and-vaults#agents-and-vaults
---
Your agents can now read the knowledge vault in chats, in questions they are asked and in the business analyst's tasks, not only while working a card. In a chat about a project they see that project's vault (or the organization vault); in a chat with no project they see the organization vault, but only on the workstation of an Owner or Admin. The vault is read-only for them: anything that belongs in it is saved through the usual commands. If the vault is not cloned on that workstation yet, the agent says so.
