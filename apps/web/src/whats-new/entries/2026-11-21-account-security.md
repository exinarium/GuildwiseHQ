---
title: Stronger sign-in and account protection
date: 2026-11-21
audience: all
help: /help/account-security
release: 1.10.0
---
Sign-in is harder to attack. After 5 wrong attempts an address pauses for 15 minutes (a browser you used in the last 30 days still gets in). A password reset of an account with two-factor login now needs a code from your authenticator or a recovery code. You get a mail on every password change and on a sign-in from a new device. Before you reveal or change secrets and keys, Guildwise asks you to **Confirm it's you** with a fresh code. Lost your authenticator and your recovery codes? Start a recovery from the sign-in page: after a mailed link and a 24-hour wait, two-factor login is removed so you can set it up again.
