title: Hand the BA a list of tickets
date: 2026-10-14
audience: admin
help: /help/intake-and-outbox#intake
---
On the Intake page, switch to **Batch** and paste many tickets, one per line. The BA works through them in the background and creates the cards without a chat per ticket. If it cannot go on with one ticket it asks on your desk, naming the ticket, and the others carry on.
