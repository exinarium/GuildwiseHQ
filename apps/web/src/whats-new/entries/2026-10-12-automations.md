---
title: Schedules are now Automations
date: 2026-10-12
audience: all
help: /help/automations-and-integrations#triggers
---
**Schedules** is now **Automations**, and an automation can start from more than the clock. It can make a card when a card reaches a stage, when a workflow's card is done, when an investigation is reported or when a ticket arrives in intake. Say which workflow, stage, card type or field value counts, fill the new card from the event with tokens such as `{card.ref}` and `{issue.title}`, and choose whether it starts as a draft or queued. A limit of cards per hour and a "no second card for the same thing" option keep it tidy. Members can set up automations that make draft cards; Owners and Admins can also queue cards directly. A workflow's builder lists the automations that start it under **Starts automatically when**. The old Schedules link still works.
