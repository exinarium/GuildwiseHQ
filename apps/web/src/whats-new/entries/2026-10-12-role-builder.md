title: A guided form for custom roles, with a prompt preview
date: 2026-10-12
audience: admin
help: /help/org-chart-and-design#custom-roles
---
Designing a **custom role** is now a guided form instead of a list of fields. On **Roles & workflows**, **New custom role** walks you through the basics, the instructions, **what the role may do** (plain-language switches with a risk note each, such as run commands or search the web), the model, seniority and skills, and a checklist of the **steps of your custom workflows** it can take, so no more typing `workflow.stage`. Problems are explained in plain words next to the section they belong to. **Preview what the agent is told** shows the instructions an agent with this role would get, from a sample card; nothing runs. Owners and Admins can design and submit roles, the desk approval is as before, and reading every organization repository stays with the Owner. The BA can still draft a role for you to check and fine-tune.
