Under **Project settings > Run and test** you can switch on integration tests (it starts off). A card then waits in Test while its workstation starts the card's branch; the tester runs your suites against it and each result shows on the card, with flaky runs marked. The copy is stopped at the verdict, or kept for a while after a failure. Switch it off for a single card in **Details**.
