---
title: Work pauses when the Claude limit is reached
date: 2026-10-15
audience: all
help: /help/troubleshooting#usage-limit
---
When your Claude subscription reaches its session or weekly limit, projects now **pause until it resets** instead of failing and retrying. The board shows "Paused until 18:00", cards keep their place in the queue, and work resumes by itself.
