Talk to your **CEO** like a colleague on your own machine: in your chat it is a real Claude Code session with your settings, skills and tools (after you run `aom-runner ceo full-power on` once on that workstation). Pick the model per message or per conversation, and if the workstation is offline the message waits and is sent when it is back. When the CEO needs a permission, you or an Admin get a desk question to authorize or deny.

You can give the CEO full power on one of your own **standing orders**, pinned to its exact wording. Say "you did X, I'd rather Y" from the chat, the Activity log, the desk or the daily report: it is remembered at once and you can undo or redo the action. The CEO uses the board first, delegates installs and repairs to DevOps (the DevOps chat is gone: ask the CEO), and the daily report shows **Things you keep asking me** when you repeat a kind of job.
