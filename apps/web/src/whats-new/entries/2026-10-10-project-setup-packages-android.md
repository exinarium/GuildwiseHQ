After the tools, DevOps now installs the project's own packages (pnpm, pip, dotnet restore and more) and, for Android and MAUI projects, the Android SDK, within the rights you ticked. While a workstation is not ready all cards of the project wait, and a notice on the desk tells you if it needs your help.
