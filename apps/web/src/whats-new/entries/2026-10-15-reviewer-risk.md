---
title: Reviewers now try to break your changes
date: 2026-10-15
audience: all
help: /help/testing-and-finalization#reviewer-risk
---
Every review now includes an adversarial pass and a risk analysis. The reviewer rates the change low, medium or high risk and says what else could break and what to test by hand. You see it on the card. A high risk makes the merge wait for your approval on the desk, and the tester and the manual QA steps look at the risky areas first. Reviewers run at high effort, and nothing lowers that automatically.
