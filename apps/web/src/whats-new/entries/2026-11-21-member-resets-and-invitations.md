**Email a reset link** is now the main way to help a member who cannot sign in. You can no longer reset the password or two-factor login of a member who also belongs to another organization; send them the link instead. Every reset you make is mailed to the member. Invitations are limited per hour, per day and in number pending, and a member who joins chooses whether their workstations work for your organization.
