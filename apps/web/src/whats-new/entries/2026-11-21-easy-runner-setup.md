The installer now asks everything on one screen, by the names of your organizations and projects, with the safe answer already filled in. Each workstation also has a small private page, **Approvals on this computer**, where you see what it allows and approve what waits. Anything that waits for your yes shows a sentence, the exact command and a **Copy** button to paste on that computer.
