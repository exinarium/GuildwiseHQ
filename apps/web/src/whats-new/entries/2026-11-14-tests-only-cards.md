---
title: Tests-only cards go to the tester
date: 2026-11-14
audience: admin
help: /help/architect#tests-only
release: 1.7.0
---
When a card only needs tests, the architect marks it as tests-only and the tester writes them, instead of a builder who is not allowed to touch the test specs. The card is no longer sent to a builder and bounced back.
