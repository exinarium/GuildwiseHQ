---
title: Your CEO now does the reporting; the BA only writes specs
date: 2026-10-22
audience: all
help: /help/ba-reporter
---
Your **CEO** now triages what agents raise, watches for trouble, writes your **daily report** and answers your scheduled tasks. The BA no longer reports to you: it turns requests and tickets into cards. The old setting **Your BA reports to you** is gone, because the CEO is always there (and **Pause** on the CEO page is the emergency stop). Items reach your desk unchanged when the CEO cannot run. Reports the BA wrote earlier stay where they are, schedules that asked the BA now ask the CEO, and the report time is on the CEO page. On **New intake** you can now tell your CEO what you need.
