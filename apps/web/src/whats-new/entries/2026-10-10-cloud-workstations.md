Add a workstation now offers your own computer, any VPS you can log in to, or a GitHub Codespace. The VPS and Codespace set themselves up and pair automatically, sleep when idle, wake when work is queued, and show an estimated monthly cost. You pay your VPS company or GitHub directly.
