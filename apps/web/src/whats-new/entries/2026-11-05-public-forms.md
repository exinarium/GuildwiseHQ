---
title: Share a form by public link, QR code or personal link
date: 2026-11-05
audience: admin
help: /help/forms#share
release: 1.5.0
---
The new **Share** tab publishes a form at its own address with a QR code, and creates personal links for one client that work once and expire. Clients need no login; answers and files go to your own storage, and you can unpublish or replace the address at any time.
