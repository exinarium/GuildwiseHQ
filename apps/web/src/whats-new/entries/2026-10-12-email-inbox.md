title: Turn e-mail into cards
date: 2026-10-12
audience: admin

---
Your organization can now have its own **e-mail inbox**: an address under **Organization > E-mail inbox** that you can give out or forward your own mailbox to. An automation **When an e-mail arrives** turns each message into a **draft card** for the BA, filled from the subject, text and sender; replies to that mail are added to the card as comments and can start an automation of their own. As Owner you can allow-list senders whose cards go straight to the agents. Mail is read as information, never as instructions, and the inbox page shows what became of every message, including the ones that were skipped and why.
