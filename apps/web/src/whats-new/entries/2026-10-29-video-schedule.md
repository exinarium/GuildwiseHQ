You now review the script of a video and schedule it, instead of waiting for a render first. Pick the days and time of day for videos and Shorts in the schedule (the time zone is yours, Johannesburg by default); Schedule gives the video the next free slot, the video is rendered shortly before it and goes public on YouTube at the slot. A preview render lets you watch it first. Shorts can list more destinations, which post once they are supported. The weekly limit of the writer follows the days you tick.
