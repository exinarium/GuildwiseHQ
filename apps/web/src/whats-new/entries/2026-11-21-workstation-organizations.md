---
title: Your workstation works for the organizations you choose
date: 2026-11-21
audience: all
help: /help/workstations-and-installer#works-for
release: 1.10.0
---
A workstation now works only for the organizations you own until you switch others on under **Works for** on the Workstations page. Nobody can move an agent onto your workstation, or hire a DevOps engineer there, without your consent.
