---
title: Pause a project by hand
date: 2026-10-18
audience: admin
---
Owners and Admins can now **pause a project** from its board. Nothing new starts on it (cards, questions, DevOps, BA work, schedules) until an Owner or Admin resumes it; shifts already running finish, and cards keep their place in the queue. Everyone on the project sees a banner with who paused it and why.
