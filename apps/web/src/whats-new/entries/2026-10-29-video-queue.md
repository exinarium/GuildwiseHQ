The Videos tab now starts from the script: read it cue by cue with its description and a caption per network, then press Schedule. A Queue shows the next two weeks with every slot, where each video is and which networks it reached, and lets you move, swap or unschedule. The schedule settings (days, time, time zone, networks) sit in their own tab, with a Re-flow button when you change the days. Shorts to TikTok ask TikTok's privacy, interaction and consent questions first.
