**New custom workflow** now opens a guided builder: start from a template (or copy a built-in workflow), list the steps, say who does each one and where a card goes next or back, and watch the diagram redraw as you type. Problems are explained in plain words next to the step, **Try a card** shows the path a card takes, and the BA can talk the draft through with you in a chat beside it. Versions can be compared step by step, and the original table editor is still there under **Advanced**.
