---
title: Review what your CEO decided
date: 2026-10-19
audience: admin
help: /help/desk#ceo-decisions
---
Decisions your CEO takes on its own, such as which working branch to keep, now appear under **CEO decisions** on the Desk. **Acknowledge** them (or **Acknowledge all**), or **Correct** a wrong one so the CEO learns from it. DevOps questions now go through the normal chain of agents before they reach you, and merge conflicts on a project that does not need your approval for merges are no longer asked.

When you **Correct** a decision, the CEO and the BA now discuss it: the BA analyses what went wrong and what it caused and proposes fix cards, the CEO replies and records the lesson in its own words, and the BA creates the fix cards. The Desk item shows the discussion as a timeline, with the status "Under review" and then "Lesson recorded · N fix cards". You do not need to do anything.
