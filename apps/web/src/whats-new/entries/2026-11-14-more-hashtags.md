Social posts and video captions now carry more hashtags (Facebook up to 5, LinkedIn up to 8, X and Bluesky up to 3) and always one standing and one popular tag next to the topical ones. Two blog writer settings, **Standing hashtags** and **Popular hashtags**, replace the lists; an empty list turns that kind of tag off.
