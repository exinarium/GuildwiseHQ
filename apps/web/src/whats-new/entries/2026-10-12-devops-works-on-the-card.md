---
title: DevOps can read and update the card it is working for
date: 2026-10-12
audience: all
help: /help/devops-and-hygiene#devops-engineer
---
A DevOps request filed for a card now carries the card: its fields, project, repository and latest comments. The DevOps engineer can comment on that card and update the fields it needs. It cannot touch any other card.
