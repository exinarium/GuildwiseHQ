title: Files on cards
date: 2026-10-13
audience: all
help: /help/board-and-cards#attachments
---
Cards can now carry files: screenshots, designs, documents. Add them on the New card form or in the **Attachments** section of a card (drag files in; images show as previews; up to 20 MB each). Builders, reviewers and testers get the files as read-only copies next to their work, so they can look at the same picture you do.
