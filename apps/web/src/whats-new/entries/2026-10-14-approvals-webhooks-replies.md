A workflow can now wait for a person: add **A person approves it on the desk** in the workflow builder; they approve, or send the card back with a comment. **Automations** can start from a private **web address** that other systems call (the secret is shown once), and its fields can fill the card. On a card made from e-mail you can **reply** to the sender from inside Guildwise, and one noisy sender can no longer use up your inbox allowance.
