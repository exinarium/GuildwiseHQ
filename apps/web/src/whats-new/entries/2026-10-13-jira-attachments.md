---
title: The BA can see Jira screenshots and files
date: 2026-10-13
audience: owner
help: /help/intake-and-outbox#jira-attachments
---
When you hand the BA a Jira ticket, it can now download the ticket's attachments through your workstation, look at the screenshots and SVG files, and attach the relevant ones to the cards it drafts. To allow it, run `guildwise-runner jira-connect` on the workstation once (the API token stays on that computer); the Workstations page shows whether Jira is connected. Runner 0.1.3 or newer is needed.
