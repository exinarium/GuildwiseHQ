The Metrics page has two new tables. **Time and cost by role** shows shifts, shift time and cost per role, seniority and model. **Builder rework by seniority, model and card size** shows how often builders' cards came back from review, so you can tell whether a stronger model pays off on large cards. Planners and architects can also mark a card large before its first build, so a senior builder takes it.
