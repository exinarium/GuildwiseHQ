title: Safer digest unsubscribe link
date: 2026-10-10
audience: all
help: /help/blog-and-digest
---
The unsubscribe link in the weekly blog digest now opens a page with an Unsubscribe button, so a mail scanner opening the link can no longer unsubscribe you by accident. Your mail program's own unsubscribe button still works in one click.
