date: 2026-10-14
audience: admin

Every organization now has a built-in **CEO** agent that answers agent questions, keeps cards moving and writes your daily report. There is nothing to appoint and no training period: it decides within your policy from the start, every decision has an Undo, and you can **Pause** it as an emergency stop. Settings are on **Organization, CEO**.
