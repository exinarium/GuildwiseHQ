**Discovery** turns an idea into a brief or PRD: an analyst interviews you one question at a time and drafts it with you; an approved PRD can go to the BA.

## Start a discovery {#start}

Sidebar, **Discovery**, **New discovery** (Owner and Admin).

1. Click **Discovery** in the sidebar (Owner and Admin) and then **New discovery**.
2. Give it a **title** and, if you like, a starting idea and a project.
3. Choose how the analyst should work: **Facilitator** (it asks, you answer), **Partner** (it also brings its own ideas, labelled as its own) or **Ideate for me** (it puts ideas and drafts on the table and you choose).
4. Choose a **CEO** option if your organization has a CEO: **Just me**, **With the CEO** (after each question the CEO proposes an answer) or **CEO prepares first** (the CEO and the analyst work through the questions, then you review every answer).
5. Optionally set a **time box** and a **budget**, pick where to start (brainstorm, stress-test, brief or PRD) and click **Start**.

## The conversation {#conversation}

Five phases: Frame, Diverge, Converge, Stress test, Capture. Click a phase to override the analyst.

- A **question card** has quick answers, a box for your own words, **Skip** and **Park for later**.
- The analyst may make a **proposal**. Nothing changes until you choose **Apply**; **Reject** drops it and **Redirect** sends your own words back to the analyst.
- When the CEO takes part, its answer is shown as **Proposed by the CEO** next to the question. **Confirm** it, **Edit** it, or answer something else yourself. The CEO never answers for you.
- Type in the box at the bottom to talk to the analyst at any time.

**Pause** stops the clock and anything new from starting. Come back the next day: nothing is lost, even if the server restarted in between.

## The panel {#panel}

Ideas, assumptions, open questions, decisions and parked items; the analyst keeps it up to date and you can edit it.

## Cost and budget {#budget}

The page shows cost and time used (also in **Costs**). With a budget, no new turn starts once it is used up; raise it to carry on.

## Draft, approve and hand to the BA {#prd}

In Capture the analyst writes the **Draft**. **Validate against the checklist**, then **Approve**; **Hand to BA** opens an intake linked to the PRD.

## Documents {#documents}

**Documents** lists every brief and PRD with status and versions.
