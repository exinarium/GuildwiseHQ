**Discovery** is where an idea becomes something that can be built. An analyst interviews you one question at a time, keeps the notes, and writes a product brief or a PRD (product requirements document) with you. When you approve the PRD you can hand it to the BA, which turns it into cards.

## Start a discovery {#start}

1. Click **Discovery** in the sidebar (Owner and Admin) and then **New discovery**.
2. Give it a **title** and, if you like, a starting idea and a project.
3. Choose how the analyst should work: **Facilitator** (it asks, you answer), **Partner** (it also brings its own ideas, labelled as its own) or **Ideate for me** (it puts ideas and drafts on the table and you choose).
4. Choose a **CEO** option if your organization has a CEO: **Just me**, **With the CEO** (after each question the CEO proposes an answer) or **CEO prepares first** (the CEO and the analyst work through the questions, then you review every answer).
5. Optionally set a **time box** and a **budget**, pick where to start (brainstorm, stress-test, brief or PRD) and click **Start**.

## The conversation {#conversation}

The session moves through five phases: Frame, Diverge, Converge, Stress test and Capture. The analyst moves it along; click a phase to override it.

- A **question card** has quick answers, a box for your own words, **Skip** and **Park for later**.
- The analyst may make a **proposal**. Nothing changes until you choose **Apply**; **Reject** drops it and **Redirect** sends your own words back to the analyst.
- When the CEO takes part, its answer is shown as **Proposed by the CEO** next to the question. **Confirm** it, **Edit** it, or answer something else yourself. The CEO never answers for you.
- Type in the box at the bottom to talk to the analyst at any time.

**Pause** stops the clock and anything new from starting. Come back the next day: nothing is lost, even if the server restarted in between.

## The panel {#panel}

Ideas, assumptions, open questions, decisions and parked items are kept in the panel beside the conversation. The analyst keeps it up to date, and you can add, edit, move or delete anything in it.

## Cost and budget {#budget}

The page shows what the session has cost so far (it is also counted in **Costs** and in the project's budget) and the time used. If you set a budget, no new turn starts once it is used up. The page says so; raise the budget to carry on. Nothing you wrote is lost.

## Draft, approve and hand to the BA {#prd}

In the Capture phase the analyst writes the document in the **Draft** panel. Use **Validate against the checklist** to check a PRD, read earlier versions with the version picker, and **Approve** it when it is ready. An approved document is copied to your vault. Click **Hand to BA** to open an intake with the PRD: the cards the BA drafts are linked to it.

## Documents {#documents}

**Documents** lists every brief and PRD with its status (draft, in review, approved, handed to the BA) and version. Open one to read it, see its versions and reopen it.
