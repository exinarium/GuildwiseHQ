**Discovery** turns an idea into a brief or PRD: an analyst interviews you one question at a time and drafts it with you; an approved PRD can go to the BA.

## Start a discovery {#start}

Sidebar, **Discovery**, **New discovery** (any member).

1. Click **Discovery** in the sidebar and then **New discovery**.
2. Give it a **title** and, if you like, a starting idea, a project and the **workstation** it runs on (one of yours).
3. Choose how the analyst should work: **Facilitator** (it asks, you answer), **Partner** (it also brings its own ideas, labelled as its own) or **Ideate for me** (it puts ideas and drafts on the table and you choose).
4. Choose a **CEO** option if your organization has a CEO: **Just me**, **With the CEO** (after each question the CEO proposes an answer) or **CEO prepares first** (the CEO and the analyst work through the questions, then you review every answer).
5. Optionally set a **time limit** and a **cost limit** (both empty means no limit), the **CEO turn cap** for "CEO prepares first" (default 12, 2 to 40), pick where to start (brainstorm, stress-test, brief or PRD) and click **Start**.

The session is yours: you manage it. Other participants can talk, add notes and see it; an Owner or Admin who is in it can override you.

## The conversation {#conversation}

Five phases: Frame, Diverge, Converge, Stress test, Capture. Click a phase to override the analyst.

- A **question card** has quick answers, a box for your own words, **Skip** and **Park for later**.
- The analyst may make a **proposal**. Nothing changes until you choose **Apply**; **Reject** drops it and **Redirect** sends your own words back to the analyst.
- When the CEO takes part, its answer is shown as **Proposed by the CEO** next to the question. **Confirm** it, **Edit** it, or answer something else yourself. The CEO never answers for you.
- Type in the box at the bottom to talk to the analyst at any time.

**Pause** stops the clock and anything new from starting. Come back the next day: nothing is lost, even if the server restarted in between.

## The panel {#panel}

Ideas, assumptions, open questions, decisions and parked items; the analyst keeps it up to date and you can edit it.

## Cost limit and time limit {#budget}

The page shows cost and time used (also in **Costs**). With a cost limit, no new turn starts once it is used up; raise it to carry on.

A **time limit** works like a meeting: a countdown shows what is left, and about 5 minutes before the end the analyst steers to wrap-up. At time-up nothing new starts and you choose **Extend** (+10, +15 or +30 minutes) or **Wrap up now** (the analyst captures where you got to). Paused time does not count. Change either limit in the session's settings.

## Guided playbook sessions {#playbook-sessions}

Type **/** and choose a playbook to open a session that follows it step by step. The page lists the playbook's steps and marks the one the analyst is on. It works like any session: answer the question cards, apply or reject proposals, and save drafts as documents. A session an agent started shows who started it and waits on your answers.

## Draft, approve and hand to the BA {#prd}

In Capture the analyst writes the **Draft**. **Validate against the checklist**, then **Approve**; **Hand to BA** opens an intake linked to the PRD.

No BA online? The hand-off waits and the intake starts when a BA's workstation is online. No BA hired at all? The page offers **Hire a BA** and the desk gets a notice.

## Documents, archive and delete {#documents}

**Documents** lists every brief and PRD with status and versions. In the session's menu, **Archive** makes it read-only (**Reopen** undoes it) and **Delete** removes it with its messages.
