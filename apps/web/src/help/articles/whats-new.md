When Guildwise HQ gets new features, a window called **What's new in Guildwise HQ** opens the next time you sign in or reload the page. It lists the changes that are meant for you, newest first, each with a short description and a **Learn more** link to the help article.

## Closing the window {#closing}

Click **Got it** (or close the window) and the changes you were shown are marked as seen. They are remembered for your account, not for your browser, so they do not come back on your phone or another computer.

## Who sees what {#who-sees-what}

You only see changes that matter for your role in the organization you are working in. Some changes are for everyone, some only for Owners, some for Owners and Admins. Platform administrators see the changes for the platform area. If you have just created your account you will not be shown changes from before you joined: the guided tour introduces the product instead.

The window never opens on top of the guided tour. It waits until the tour is closed.

## Read the list again {#read-again}

Open **Help** and choose **See what's new** on the What's new card. The list shows every change for your role, including the ones you have already seen. Closing it there does not change anything.
