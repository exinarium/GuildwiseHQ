The **Integrations** page lists each login a workstation needs.

## Connect {#connect}

Open **Integrations** (sidebar, Organization) and pick a workstation if you have several. Each login is a tile: Claude has **one tile per account**, and **Connect another account** adds one. Click **Connect** (or **Replace**) on a tile; your token is encrypted in your browser for that workstation. MCP servers show **Sign in** (or **Sign in again**) and **Sign out**, and every tile has **Test**.

## Greyed out {#disabled}

The tile says why. **From a terminal** shows the command instead.
