You can chat with any agent to ask questions, offer suggestions, discuss blockers or decisions about the card they are working on. Chat is saved as a thread and linked to the card's history.

## Starting a chat {#starting-a-chat}

There are two ways to start a chat with an agent:

1. **From a card**: Click the card on the board, then click **Chat**. A live conversation opens with the agent (if the workstation is connected). The chat is tied to that card.

2. **From the desk**: If an agent asked a question on the desk, click **Chat** on the desk item to reply in the same thread.

## Threads {#threads}

Each chat is a thread. You can:

- Ask questions about the card.
- Offer suggestions.
- Discuss blockers or decisions.

The agent sees your messages in its next shift and can reply. Chat is saved to a thread and appears in the card's history.

A chat that belongs to a project is marked "visible to this project's managers": other managers of that project can read it and join in.

Rights are checked on every message: if you are removed from the project, you can no longer chat with its agents through an old thread.

**Note**: agents may write notes and drafts in their own scratch folder during a chat. They cannot change your repositories, the vault or their own configuration from a chat.

## Asking the BA {#asking-the-ba}

You can also ask the BA in any chat or start a new one from the desk's **Chat** button. Ask the BA to:

- Create cards: "create a card for the login form".
- Specify cards from tickets: "what should we do about this issue?".
- Run a task: "check our logs for errors".
- Answer a question: "should we use Postgres or MongoDB?".

The BA works with your rights: it can only use projects you are a member of. It reads the ledger, desk, vault and repositories (never changes anything), and posts one report on your desk with the conversation linked.

Other agents cannot create cards from chat; only the BA can.

## Archiving chats {#archiving-chats}

You can archive old chats to keep things tidy. Archived chats still appear in the card's history but are hidden from the chat list. Click the menu on a thread to archive or unarchive it.
