Owners and Admins use **Capabilities**.

## The registry {#registry}

It lists every skill, persona and playbook and where it comes from: built in, your vault, a project or a pack. Pick a role's skills on Roles and workflows.

## Packs {#packs}

Add a pack by git URL and tag, or click **Add BMAD Method**. It starts untrusted: nothing of it is used. **Review and approve** shows its files, scripts flagged, and approves that exact commit. Scripts stay off unless you enable them, and only run in the sandbox. Pack text reaches agents as outside content. **Check for updates** shows a diff to accept.
