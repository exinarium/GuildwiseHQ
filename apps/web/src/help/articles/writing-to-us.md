**Contact us** is where you reach our support team. Your request is tracked and you can see the answer when we reply.

## Contact us {#contact-us}

Open **Contact us** from:

- The user menu (click your name at the bottom of the sidebar).
- The **Contact us** tab on the Help page.
- The command palette (press Ctrl K and type "contact").
- The sign-in page footer (if you cannot sign in yet).

To send a message:

1. Choose what it is about: bug report, question, feature request or billing (payments are explained in [Billing](/help/billing-and-plans)).
2. Write a subject and a message.
3. Optionally attach a screenshot (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2.5 MB).
4. When opening from the menu or palette, the page you were on is captured first. Look at it, remove it, or upload a different image.
5. Click **Send**.

Your name, email and organization are included so we can answer and look at your account.

Right after you send it you get a confirmation email with your reference (for example SUP-1001), what happens next and when to expect an answer (within 24 to 48 hours).

## What we send with your message {#what-we-receive}

Open **What we send with your request** to see exactly what goes along:

- The page you were on.
- The app version.
- Your browser, screen size and time zone.
- The last few errors from your browser, up to ten (untick the box to leave them out).

Your name, email and organization are added automatically.

## My requests {#my-requests}

We answer by email, and the answer also appears under **Help > My requests**.

- **Open**: we have not answered yet.
- **Answered**: we have replied; you can write again if you want.
- **Closed**: we are done.

If you write again on an answered or closed request, it reopens. Requests where our reply is the last message show a "New reply" badge, and the My requests tab shows how many are new.

You can reply in the app or just read our answer there.

## Cannot sign in {#cannot-sign-in}

If you cannot sign in:

1. Click **Contact us** at the bottom of the sign-in page.
2. Give your name and email.
3. Write your message.

We answer by email and can help you access your account. To reset a forgotten password yourself, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page, or ask your Owner or an Admin for a temporary password.
