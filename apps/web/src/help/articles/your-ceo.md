The **CEO** is an optional agent that works your desk and the floor for you: it answers agent questions, keeps cards moving and writes your daily report. The Owner appoints it on **Organization, CEO** (one of your workstations hosts it). You can pause it any time.

## Training period {#training}

A new CEO starts in training. It **recommends** and you decide: during training, hiring, benching, retiring, promoting, model changes and scaling are only recommendations, and your answers on the desk teach it. The Overview tab shows how often you agreed with it. When you are happy, press **Graduate**: the CEO then decides what your policy allows. You can extend training by 7 days or restart it later.

## What it may decide {#authority}

The **Authority** tab sets, for each kind of action, whether the CEO decides, recommends or never does it. Matters like production deploys, manual QA and budgets stay yours unless you delegate one (you confirm each). Billing, members, security and secrets are never delegated. Below a minimum confidence it recommends instead.

## Activity log and Undo {#activity}

Everything it decided, recommended or refused is in the **Activity log**, with its reason, its confidence and the evidence. Most decisions have an **Undo**, also on the desk item and in the daily report. The Owner can export the log.

## Memory {#memory}

It remembers rules you give it. Tick **Remember this for the CEO** when you answer, or add a rule on the **Memory** tab. Proposed rules wait for your **Keep** or **Drop**. Only the Owner can read the memory.

## Talking to it {#talking}

Press **Ask** in the top bar for your standing chat, or give it a task on the **Tasks** tab. Its spend shows as **Management** on the Costs page.
