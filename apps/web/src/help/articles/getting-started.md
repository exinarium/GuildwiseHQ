When you sign in for the first time, Guildwise HQ shows you around and introduces the main areas you will work in. You can replay the tour any time from the Help menu.

## First login {#first-login}

1. Check your email for an invitation link. It comes either from the platform administrator if you are starting a new organization, or from the Owner or an Admin if you are joining an existing one.
2. Click the link, choose your name and a password (12 or more characters). If you already have an account, sign in instead and confirm.
3. You are now logged in and can access the dashboard.

Every account belongs to an organization. There are no standalone accounts except for the platform administrator.

## The tour {#the-tour}

The first time you sign in, Guildwise HQ starts a guided tour of the main pages: the board where your cards live, the desk where you answer questions, intake from external systems, and more. The tour takes you to each area, shows you what is there and what you can do.

You can play the tour again any time: open **Help** in the sidebar (or press Ctrl K and type "tour").

## The sidebar {#the-sidebar}

The left side of the app is split into areas. Each area groups related pages. From the top:

- **Work**: The board (all your open cards), the desk (questions waiting for you), intake (tickets from Jira and GitHub), schedules (automatic actions), and your projects.
- **Knowledge**: The ledger (decisions and standing orders), interests (topics you watch), vaults and shared knowledge.
- **Organization**: The org chart (who works for you), workstations (computers where your agents run), agents (hiring and firing), roles and workflows (custom organization design).
- **Analytics**: Costs, metrics, and where your team's money goes.
- **Admin**: Organization settings, member list, security, and plan information.
- **Help**: The help centre, contact form and your message history.

At the top of the sidebar, a button switches between organizations if you belong to several. Below each section, click an item to open it.

On a phone, press the menu button at the top left to open and close the sidebar.

## Command palette {#command-palette}

Press **Ctrl K** (Windows, Linux) or **Cmd K** (macOS) anywhere in the app to open the command palette: a search box that finds pages, actions and help topics.

Type what you are looking for: "board", "hire agents", "contact", or the name of a project. Results appear as you type and include pages, contacts, cards and articles. Press Enter to go to the first result, or use the arrow keys to pick one.

## Keyboard shortcuts {#keyboard-shortcuts}

Press **?** anywhere to see a list of all keyboard shortcuts. A few essential ones:

- **Ctrl K** or **Cmd K**: open the command palette.
- **n**: create a new card.
- **g** then **b**: go to the board.
- **g** then **d**: go to the desk.
- **g** then **a**: go to agents.
- **g** then **p**: go to projects.
- **g** then **w**: go to workstations.
- **g** then **i**: go to interests.
- **/**: search the page you are on.

## What's on this page {#page-help}

Many pages in Guildwise HQ have a **What's on this page** panel. Open it by pressing **?** on that page, or look for a help icon. The panel lists every control and area of the page and what it does: useful when you first visit a page or need a reminder about a button.

The panel also links to related help articles if you want to read more.
