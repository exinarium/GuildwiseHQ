When you sign in for the first time, Guildwise HQ shows you around and introduces the main areas you will work in. You can replay the tour any time from the Help page.

## The overview video {#overview-video}

If you want the whole picture first, the **Overview** video is a tour of about twelve minutes: the team of agents, the board, your desk, Discovery and the Boardroom, workstations, control and releases. The section videos in Help then teach each area in depth.

## The training video {#training-video}

On the **Help** page, the **Getting started** video is a spoken tutorial of under three minutes. A friendly voice takes you from an empty organization to a team of agents at work while you watch it happen on screen:

1. **The Get started checklist:** a new organization shows **Get started** in the sidebar, with what is left to do. Each step ticks itself off.
2. **Add a workstation:** on **Workstations**, **Add a workstation** asks where your agents run, gives you one command to copy and shows the progress live.
3. **Create a project:** on **Projects**, **New project** with a name and a short key; then add the repository in the project settings.
4. **Hire a lean team:** on **Agents**, **Hire agent** for each role, with the project selected under **Where may this agent work**. A lean team has seven roles: a business analyst, a planner, an architect, a builder, a pre-reviewer, a reviewer and a tester.
5. **Create a card and talk to the BA:** **New card** at the top of the sidebar, then **Specify**; or open the chat, pick the business analyst and ask in plain words. The analyst can turn the conversation into cards.
6. **Answer on the Desk:** when an agent needs a decision it lands on the **Desk**, and the number in the sidebar tells you. Pick an option and choose **Answer**.
7. **Watch the board:** the card moves from column to column by itself as the agents plan, design, build, review and test.
8. **Help and shortcuts:** **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar, the guided tour, and Ctrl K to jump anywhere.

The captions show everything the voice says and are on by default; use the **CC** button on the player to turn them off. Under the video, each step is a button that jumps to that part.

## Videos {#videos}

There are 30 short videos, one per screen, in the Help centre and on the website. Each runs one to two minutes, with a voice-over and captions.

## Your settings {#your-settings}

**Settings** (sidebar, bottom) is your own account: your picture, your time zone, **Notifications** (push notifications on your devices, with quiet hours), the **Monday briefing**, **Two-factor login** (**Set up**), the weekly blog digest and expert mode for code changes. The time of the CEO's daily report is not here: the Owner sets it on the CEO's **Reporting & alerts** tab. Organization-wide settings are on **Organization**, next to it.

## First login {#first-login}

There are two ways in:

- **Start your own organization.** If you use a hosted service, click its sign-up button on the website and fill in your organization name, your name, email and a password (12 or more characters), then open the link in the confirmation email. A hosted service may ask you to add a card to start a free trial first; see [Billing](/help/billing-and-plans#trial). If you run Guildwise HQ yourself, the platform account invites people to start an organization (see the self-hosting guide).
- **Join with an invitation.** Open the invitation email. It comes from the Owner or an Admin of the organization you join (or from the platform administrator for a new organization). Click the link and choose your name and a password. If you already have an account, sign in instead and confirm.

After you sign in you land in the app, and the guided tour starts. For a new sign-up that is right after the card is saved: until then the tour, the Get started checklist and What's new wait.

## Forgot your password {#forgot-password}

On the sign-in page click **Forgot your password?** and type your email. We send a link that works once and expires after an hour. Choose a new password (12 or more characters); you are signed out everywhere else, then sign in with the new one. If you use two-factor login you still enter your code. We answer the same way for every address, so the page never tells whether an email has an account. Platform administrators can use it too. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder, or ask your Owner or an Admin for a temporary password (they find it under **Organization, Members**); you then choose your own password when you sign in.

Every account belongs to an organization. There are no standalone accounts except for the platform administrator.

## The tour {#the-tour}

The first time you sign in, Guildwise HQ starts a guided tour. Its **first step is the getting-started video** described above, right in the tour card, with captions and the chapter buttons; press **Next: take the tour** (or **Skip tour**) when you are done, and the video pauses. If the video is not available the step shows a short text instead. The rest of the tour covers the main pages: the board where your cards live, the desk where you answer questions, intake from external systems, **Ask DevOps** for help with your workstation, and more. The tour takes you to each area, shows you what is there and what you can do.

You can play the tour again any time: open **Help** in the sidebar and click **Replay the tour** (or press Ctrl K and choose **Take the tour**). The tour is shown once, and you can watch the video again any time from **Help**. If you close it half way it does not come back by itself; **Help** offers to resume it where you stopped. After an update, the [What's new](/help/whats-new) window tells you what changed; it never opens on top of the tour.

## The Get started checklist {#get-started}

Until your organization is set up, the sidebar shows a **Get started** button with your progress (for example 2/5). Click it for the list:

1. **Create your first project.**
2. **Add its repository** (the code repository the agents work in).
3. **Pair a workstation**: the machine that runs your agents. It can be your own computer, a server (VPS) or a GitHub Codespace (see [Workstations and the installer](/help/workstations-and-installer#add-a-workstation) and [Cloud workstations](/help/cloud-workstations)). Its DevOps engineer is hired for you.
4. **Hire your team**: a business analyst, a builder, a reviewer and a tester (see [Agents](/help/agents#hiring)).
5. **Talk to the business analyst**: describe something you need, in a chat or an intake, and it turns it into cards (see [Chat with agents](/help/chat-with-agents#asking-the-ba)).

Each step has a button that opens the right page, with the form already open where there is one. The first two steps are for Owners and Admins; anyone can do the others. You never tick anything yourself: a step shows as done as soon as it is true, whoever did it and wherever. When all five are done the button disappears.

Everyone in the organization sees the same progress. Members cannot create projects or add repositories, so those two steps show "An Owner or Admin does this step" for them. Click **Hide this checklist** to put it away; it stays hidden for you on every device. To bring it back, open **Help** and click **Show the checklist**.

The guided tour mentions the checklist at its last step, so the two do not compete: the tour shows you around, the checklist tells you what is still left to set up.

## The sidebar {#the-sidebar}

The left side of the app is split into areas. Each area groups related pages. From the top:

- **Work**: Board (the current project's cards), Backlog (ideas not yet cards), Environments (where a code project's app runs), Research (only on projects with research notes), Desk (questions waiting for you), Intake (tickets from Jira and GitHub; Owner and Admin), Automations (automatic actions), and Projects.
- **Organization**: Agents, Org chart, CEO (Owner and Admin), Roles & workflows (custom organization design), Workstations (the computers where your agents run), Releases, Integrations, Metrics and Costs (Costs is for Owner and Admin).
- **Knowledge**: Interests (topics you watch, when switched on), Ledger (decisions and standing orders) and Vaults (Owner and Admin).
- **Bottom of the sidebar**: the **Get started** button while your organization is being set up, **Settings** (your own account), **Organization** (members, plan, billing and security of the organization) and **Help**. Your name there opens the user menu with theme, **Your settings**, **Help**, **Contact us**, **My requests** and **Log out**.

The bar at the top of every page has the search box (the command palette), the **?** button (what is on this page), **Ask DevOps** (see [DevOps and repository hygiene](/help/devops-and-hygiene#devops-engineer)) and **Chat** (see [Chat with agents](/help/chat-with-agents)).

At the top of the sidebar, the organization switcher lets you change organizations if you belong to several.

On a phone, press the menu button at the top left to open and close the sidebar.

## Command palette {#command-palette}

Press **Ctrl K** (Windows, Linux) or **Cmd K** (macOS) anywhere in the app to open the command palette: a search box that finds pages, actions and help topics.

Type what you are looking for: "board", "new card", "contact", or the name of a project, card or agent. Results appear as you type and include actions, pages, projects, cards, agents and desk items. Press Enter to go to the first result, or use the arrow keys to pick one.

## Keyboard shortcuts {#keyboard-shortcuts}

Press **?** anywhere (when you are not typing in a field) to see a list of all keyboard shortcuts. A few essential ones:

- **Ctrl K** or **Cmd K**: open the command palette.
- **n**: create a new card.
- **g** then **b**: go to the board.
- **g** then **d**: go to the desk.
- **g** then **a**: go to agents.
- **g** then **p**: go to projects.
- **g** then **w**: go to workstations.
- **g** then **i**: go to interests.
- **/**: search cards (focuses the board search, or opens the command palette).

## What's on this page {#page-help}

Many pages in Guildwise HQ have a **What's on this page** panel. Open it with the **?** button in the top bar (or choose **What is on this page** in the command palette). The panel lists every control and area of the page and what it does: useful when you first visit a page or need a reminder about a button.

The panel also links to related help articles if you want to read more.
