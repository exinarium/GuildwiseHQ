### Intake and outbox
Intake from Jira and GitHub, unmapped tickets, credentials, the outbox and guardrails.
- Intake: To bring a ticket into Guildwise HQ:
- Unmapped tickets: If a ticket does not match a project (for example it is a support request for a system you do not track):
- Jira attachments: The tool your BA reads Jira with shows the names of a ticket's attachments, but not the screenshots or design files themselves, and agents have no internet. So the BA asks your workstation's runner to download them with your Jira login.…
- Credentials: - Jira: on the workstation run guildwise-runner jira-connect. It asks for your Jira address, your Atlassian e-mail and an API token (create one at id.atlassian.com, Security, API tokens;…
- Outbox: When an agent drafts an update to post back to a ticket (a comment, a status change, etc.), it lands on your desk as an Outbound update.
- Guardrails: Before posting, your runner checks the text against your organization's agent names and internal terms. A draft that mentions an agent by name, or gives away internal process details (review scores, rework reasons and similar), is refused even if you approved it.…
