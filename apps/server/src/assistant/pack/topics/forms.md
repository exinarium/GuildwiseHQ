### Forms
Design a form with drag and drop, set the fields and their rules, add your company logo, preview it as a client sees it, and keep every saved version.
- Build a form: Sidebar, Forms, New form (Owner and Admins; everyone else can look at forms and their preview).
- Field types and properties: There are ten field types: short text, long text, email, phone, number, date, dropdown, radio buttons, checkboxes and file upload.
- Preview: The Preview tab shows the form exactly as a client will see it, with the same checks: an empty required field, an invalid email, a number outside its range, a date outside its range, and a file of the wrong type or over the size limit each show a clear message next to the field.…
- Versions: Every save creates a new version. Earlier versions stay readable: open the Versions menu and pick one to look at it (it opens read-only). Saving without any change does not create a version.…
- Company logo: In the form settings choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP image of up to 512 KB. It is shown at the top of the form and of the preview. SVG images are not accepted. The logo is your organisation's branding picture, not client data; client answers and uploads never go to Guildwise.
- Share a form: A saved form has a Share tab (Owners and Admins). Publish gives the form a public address (/f/ and a long random id) with a QR code of exactly that address, to download as PNG or SVG. Publishing needs a storage connection for the answers and for every file field.…
- Personal links: Under Personal links create a link for one client or record: type a reference (use a client number, not a name) and choose 7 to 90 days. The link works once, can be revoked, and carries no personal data.…
- When your workstation is offline: The public form shows an email box and sends nothing but the address. When the workstation has been back for 30 seconds the client gets one email with a secure, single-use link, and your desk gets one notice per case with Resend link.
