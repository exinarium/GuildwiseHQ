### Governance and audit
What an agent may do, approval gates, approving from a notification, exporting the audit log, and offboarding a person.
- What it may do: Open an agent's card and see in one place its tools, the repositories and projects it can reach, its provider and which key pays, delegation grants, playbooks, skills and packs, its cost and the budgets that apply.
- Approval gates: Large changes, DevOps actions and outgoing posts wait for a person. Agents propose; you decide.
- Approve from a notification: A push for an approval has Approve and Reject buttons, and a question with options shows its first two options. A button opens a page that asks you to sign in and waits for you to press it. The link works once, for 15 minutes, only for you.
- The audit log: Every claim, answer and approval is an event that is only ever added to. Owners and Admins can export it.
- Offboarding: Removing a person runs a checklist: notification links of the organization are revoked and the organization's packs come off their workstations. A report shows what was done.
