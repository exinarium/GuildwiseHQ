### Your CEO
The built-in agent you talk to first: it runs your desk and the floor, works on your own workstation when you ask, and learns from your corrections.
- Full power in your own chat: When you talk to the CEO it is a real Claude Code session on your workstation: your own settings, skills and tools, your home directory and no Guildwise sandbox.…
- Pick the model per message: The chip in the chat composer shows the model ("Default (Sonnet)"). Choose another for this message or for this conversation. Switching the model inside the same runtime keeps the session; another runtime starts a new session with a short handover.…
- Orders it carries out with full power: On the Ledger you can give the CEO full power on one of your own standing orders. The order is pinned to its exact wording: any edit voids it unless you keep full power while editing.…
- What it may decide: The Authority tab sets, for each kind of action, whether the CEO decides, recommends or never does it. Matters like production deploys, manual QA and budgets stay yours unless you delegate one (you confirm each). Billing, members, security and secrets are never delegated.…
- Questions from agents and escalation: When an agent asks a blocking question, the system routes it. With an active CEO that can be reached, the question goes to the CEO and the BA is skipped. If the CEO cannot be reached (its workstation is offline, or you paused it), the question goes to your desk.…
- Decisions you review: Every decision the CEO takes on its own that you would otherwise have been asked about (answers to questions routed to it, matters you delegated, such as which working branch a repository uses) is listed on the desk under CEO decisions.…
- Activity log, Undo and Correct: Everything it decided, recommended or refused is in the Activity log, with its reason, its confidence and the evidence. Most decisions have an Undo, also on the desk item and in the daily report. The Owner can export the log.
- Memory: It remembers rules you give it. Tick Remember this for the CEO when you answer, or add a rule on the Memory tab. Proposed rules wait for your Keep or Drop. Only the Owner can read the memory.
- Talking to it: Press Ask in the top bar for your standing chat, or give it a task on the Tasks tab. Its spend shows as Management on the Costs page. The DevOps engineer no longer has a chat of its own: ask the CEO, who delegates to DevOps and tells you what happened.…
