### Pricing
What Guildwise HQ costs: nothing, and there are no plans to choose from. Free to self-host, and free on our hosted instance for any number of people, subject to fair use.
- What is free: Everything. The software is free and open source under Apache 2.0: every feature, unlimited organizations, any use. Self-hosting costs nothing but your own time and server. Guildwise Cloud, our hosted instance, is free to use too, for any number of people, subject to fair use.…
- Donations: Hosting it costs money. If Guildwise is useful to you, donations through Ko-fi (ko-fi.com/guildwisehq) or GitHub Sponsors (github.com/sponsors/GuildwiseHQ) help cover running costs. Everyone gets the same thing.
- Backups and your data: Guildwise Cloud does not include backups or data maintenance. We do not own your data, so we do not keep copies of it. Export it whenever you like.
- Setup and customization: Help with setup, custom workflows and agents is community help, through GitHub issues and discussions.
