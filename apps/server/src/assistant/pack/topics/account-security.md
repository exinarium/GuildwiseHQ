### Account security
Sign-in protection and locks, password reset with a code, security mails, Confirm it's you, requiring two-factor login and the platform Security page.
- Sign-in protection: Wrong answers are counted: a wrong password, a wrong two-factor code, a wrong answer to Confirm it's you and a wrong password reset code.
- Password reset needs a code: Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page. If your account has two-factor login, the reset page also asks for a code: a 6-digit code from your authenticator app, or one of your recovery codes. Someone who only has your mailbox cannot take the account.
- Security mails: You are mailed when something sensitive happens on your account:
- Confirm it's you: Before a sensitive action Guildwise asks Confirm it's you: "Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (or a recovery code) to continue." Accounts without two-factor login are asked "Enter your password to continue." After a correct answer the action carries on by itself.
- Require two-factor login: The Require two-factor login setting forces everyone to use two-factor login on the whole server. It is off by default.
