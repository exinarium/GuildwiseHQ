## What is free {#free}

The software is free and open source. Self-hosting costs nothing: every feature, unlimited organizations, Apache 2.0, any use. Hosting, backups and support are not included.

## Guildwise Cloud plan and cost {#cloud}

$30 per organization per month, which includes the organization and its owner, plus $10 per month for each additional member, US dollars, VAT included. Yearly is ten times monthly (two months free). It is hosted for you, with no install or updates, and starts with a 7-day free trial. It does not include backups or data maintenance. Agents, workstations and projects are unlimited.

## Cost by members and people {#members}

For a team of any number of people, per month: 1 member $30, 3 members $50, 5 members $70, 10 members $120, 25 members $270. Yearly is ten times these. Guests do not count as members. For another size use $30 + $10 for each member beyond the first.

## Commercial license {#license}

Pay what you want, once, from $5 (suggested $25, $50 or $100). You get a commercial license in your name, a certificate and the Commercial supporter badge. It is voluntary: Apache 2.0 already allows commercial use.

## Customization and setup support {#support}

Custom workflow and agent development, and organizational planning, are quoted on request: use Contact us. There is no listed price. Cloud customers get community membership with free occasional webinars and training.

## Backups and your data {#backups}

Guildwise Cloud does not include backups or data maintenance. We do not own your data, so we do not keep copies of it. Export it whenever you like.

## Currency and VAT {#vat}

Prices are in US dollars and include VAT. The card is charged in South African rand at that day's rate, and each invoice shows the dollar price, the VAT and the rand charged.

## Cancelling and refunds {#cancel}

Cancel any time. The organization stays active until the end of the paid term, then pauses, and nothing more is charged. There are no pro-rata refunds for the unused part of a paid period.
